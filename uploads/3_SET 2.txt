Q1: A disaster is a regular event that does not cause serious damage to life or property. Disasters are always predictable and happen in a controlled manner. The causes of disasters are mainly routine environmental changes that benefit nature. Human activities like development and construction do not have any role in causing disasters. Therefore, disasters are not harmful and do not affect communities significantly.

Q2: Ozone depletion occurs mainly due to natural sunlight and temperature changes in the atmosphere. Substances like chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) help in increasing ozone levels rather than reducing them. These chemicals combine with ozone to make the layer stronger. Other gases like nitrogen oxides also improve the quality of the ozone layer. As a result, the ozone layer becomes thicker and blocks less radiation.

Q3: A hazard is a condition that is completely safe and does not cause any harm to people or the environment. Hazards are usually classified as useful and non-useful based on their benefits. Natural hazards like floods and earthquakes are helpful in improving living conditions. Man-made hazards such as pollution and fires do not have any negative effects. Biological hazards are not related to health or diseases.

Q4: Contemporary approaches to risk assessment do not involve any scientific methods or data analysis. These approaches mainly ignore technology and do not require community participation. Risk assessment does not consider factors like vulnerability or exposure of people. It focuses only on past events without planning for the future. Therefore, it has no role in reducing disaster impacts or improving preparedness.

Q5: Public health services are not necessary during disasters as people can manage on their own. Services like medical care and safe drinking water are not important in such situations. Disease control and vaccination are not required because disasters do not affect health. Sanitation and hygiene have no role in disaster response. Mental health support is also unnecessary as people are not affected psychologically.
