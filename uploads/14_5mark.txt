Q16(a):Disaster response means the actions taken after a disaster to help people who are affected and to reduce their problems. It includes rescuing people who are trapped or in danger and giving them first aid. People are also provided with basic needs like food, water, and shelter so that they can survive safely. Medical help is given to injured people, and steps are taken to prevent diseases from spreading. In addition, efforts are made to rebuild houses and help people return to their normal lives. The government and different organizations work together to manage the situation and provide support. Overall, disaster response is about helping people during difficult times and making sure they recover as soon as possible.
