Q16(a):Disaster response refers to the immediate, well-planned, and coordinated actions taken to ensure safety, minimize damage, and support affected populations after a disaster occurs. It includes multiple types such as emergency response, where search and rescue operations, evacuation, and first aid are carried out by trained personnel like fire services and disaster response teams. Relief response focuses on providing essential needs such as food, clean drinking water, shelter, and clothing through organized camps. Medical response involves treatment of injuries, prevention of disease outbreaks, vaccination, and mental health care for victims. Logistical response ensures efficient transportation and distribution of resources and personnel. Rehabilitation response helps restore normal life by rebuilding homes, infrastructure, and providing livelihood support. Effective coordination, communication, and psychological assistance are also key components of a successful disaster response system.
