Q1: A disaster is an event that causes damage to people and property. It can be natural or man-made. Causes include earthquakes, floods, and human activities like pollution and poor planning. These factors lead to harmful situations.

Q2: Ozone depletion is caused by harmful gases released by human activities. Chemicals like CFCs from machines and sprays damage the ozone layer. Other gases also affect it. This reduces its ability to protect the Earth.


Q3: A hazard is something that can cause harm. There are different types like natural hazards and man-made hazards. Some hazards are also related to diseases. These can affect people and the environment.

Q4: Risk assessment is the process of identifying risks. Modern methods use data and technology to understand risks. It also includes people’s involvement. This helps in reducing disaster effects.

Q5: Public health services include medical care, clean water, sanitation, disease control, vaccination, and support services. These help people during disasters. They reduce health problems and risks.
